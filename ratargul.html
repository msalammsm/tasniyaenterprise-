Ratargul Swamp Forest Travel Guide: Bangladesh's Only Freshwater Swamp

Drift by boat through a submerged forest — the 'Sundarbans of Sylhet'.

Ratargul is Bangladesh's only freshwater swamp forest — a haunting, beautiful maze of flooded trees best explored by boat in the monsoon.

Overview

Ratargul, about 26 km from Sylhet city, is the only freshwater swamp forest in Bangladesh and one of the few in the world. Maintained by the Forest Department, the forest stays underwater for five to six months each year: in peak monsoon the hijal and koroch trees stand in up to 20 feet of water, and the only way in is by boat.

Gliding silently between submerged trunks, with green canopy overhead and bird calls all around, feels like entering another world. A watch tower near the forest edge gives a panoramic view over the flooded treetops, and kayaks are available for those who want to paddle the maze themselves.

Top Things to Do in Ratargul

Take a wooden boat deep into the submerged forest — the essential Ratargul experience. Climb the watch tower for the full panorama. Rent a kayak (about BDT 150 per person, three per kayak) to roam at your own pace, and hire a local guide (around BDT 150) the first time as the channels are confusing. Birdwatchers will find the monsoon forest alive with species.

How to Get There

From Ambarkhana in Sylhet, shared CNGs (about BDT 80 per person) run via Airport Road; a reserved CNG return trip is BDT 800–1,200, and a private car or microbus is BDT 2,000–3,000 per day. Boats are hired at Motor Ghat (about BDT 1,500) or Chowrangi Ghat (BDT 700–800) — kayakers should start from Chowrangi. Ratargul can also be visited en route to Jaflong via the Saraighat–Gowainghat road.

Travel Tips

  • Monsoon is the season — in winter water drops to around 10 feet and much of the magic is gone.
  • Take the boat early morning for calm water and the best light.
  • Hire a guide on your first visit; the channels all look alike.
  • Carry mosquito repellent and drinking water.
  • Confirm boat fares before boarding, especially on holidays.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Ratargul?

The monsoon (June to September), when the forest is submerged under up to 20 feet of water and boats glide between the treetops.

How much does a boat cost at Ratargul?

Roughly BDT 1,500 from Motor Ghat or BDT 700–800 from Chowrangi Ghat per boat; kayaks are about BDT 150 per person.

Are there hotels at Ratargul?

No — visitors stay in Sylhet city, which has everything from budget to 5-star accommodation, and visit Ratargul as a half-day trip.
